Let $x_0, x_1, \ldots$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $x_0 = 0$, $x_1 = 1$, and for each integer $k \geq 2$, there exists an integer $1 \leq t \leq k$ such that
\[ x_k = \frac{x_{k-1} + \dots + x_{k-t}}{t}. \]
Find the minimum possible value of $x_{2024} - x_{2025}$.

Please reason step by step, and put your final answer within \boxed{}.
